Write it, understand it, remember it

Children trace and write with clear guides, find out what every word means, then write it again from memory. Practice stays on the device: no accounts, no uploads, no ads.

What it does

A patient handwriting tutor for small hands: clear guides, honest feedback, and now a way to learn what the words mean in whichever language your child is writing.

Write in 8 languages

Choose the language your child practises separately from the app language: English, French, German, Spanish, Romanian, Japanese hiragana, a starter set of 60 Simplified Chinese characters, or Hindi in Devanagari. Stroke-by-stroke steps stay in your language.

Letter formation, step by step

Clear models and guides for print and joined cursive in Latin letters, plus native stroke order for kana, Chinese characters and Devanagari. Latin letters follow a handwriting-family order (c, o, a, d…) with a plain A-to-Z option.

Feedback after every attempt

Each attempt is scored for shape, spacing, line control, pressure and rhythm. Common slips, such as a mirrored b or d, are named in plain words so your child knows what to fix.

Word Explorer

See what every word and sentence means, hear it at normal or slow speed with pinyin, romaji or Hindi romanisation, and read short grammar notes and memory tricks. Tap any word to practise writing it.

Meaning Quiz

Ten quick questions: read it, hear it, then write it from memory. Words come back on later days until they stick.

Stories, postcards and more

Silly stories, postcards, comics, wanted posters, fluency drills and free writing turn practice into real writing. Every activity saves progress.

Honest feedback, never a fake gold star

Many handwriting apps reward any scribble. Pen Licence tells a child what went well and what to try next.

  • Scores for shape, spacing, line control, pressure and rhythm after every attempt.
  • Mirror letters and other common slips are named in plain words.
  • Pressure coaching with Apple Pencil; finger writers are never marked down for pressure.
  • Each language remembers exactly where your child stopped.

Who it's for

Pen Licence is built for parents, teachers and therapists looking for a kids handwriting app, a letter tracing app for iPhone and iPad, or a way to learn to write in another language. It fits families practising at home, bilingual families and heritage-language learners, and classrooms or therapy sessions where letter formation matters.

Home practice

A calm place for children from about age 4 to build letter confidence a few minutes at a time, then move on to words and sentences.

Bilingual families

Keep the app in your home language and practise writing another one, or the other way round. Steps and meanings stay in the language you read.

Classroom and therapy

Formation and pressure feedback, mirror-letter catches and a PDF practice summary can support a conversation with a teacher or occupational therapist.

Curious young writers

Hiragana, Chinese characters and Devanagari each come with their own guides and stroke order, and every word can be heard slowly.

Frequently asked questions

What age group is it for?

Children from about age 4 learning their first letters, through confident writers of 8 and up working on words, sentences and joined cursive. Older learners picking up a new script can use it too.

Do we need an Apple Pencil?

No. Finger practice works on iPhone and iPad. Apple Pencil on iPad adds pressure coaching; without one, pressure never counts against your child.

Is there a subscription?

No. Try Pen Licence free for 7 days, then unlock it with a one-time in-app purchase. No subscription, no ads.
