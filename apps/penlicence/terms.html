Inkla gives practice observations only. It is not an assessment of any learning difficulty. If you have concerns, speak to your child's teacher.

2. Service Description

Inkla is a handwriting practice app for iPhone and iPad. It provides stroke-formation feedback, an on-device curriculum, and — when an Apple Pencil is used — pressure coaching. The app runs entirely offline. We do not receive or store your data.

3. Intended Audience

Inkla is designed for children learning to form letters, typically ages 4–8, under parent or teacher supervision. We do not receive or store your child's data. These terms are addressed to parents, guardians and teachers; you are responsible for your child's use of Inkla.

4. Feedback and Scoring

The app's stroke-recognition and pressure-coaching feedback is intended as a practice aid, not a clinical assessment. Scores reflect consistency with a chosen letter model and stroke order. Inkla does not diagnose handwriting difficulties, motor delays, or any medical condition.

5. Apple Pencil

Pressure metrics are computed only from Apple Pencil input. Finger input does not produce reliable pressure data and is silently excluded from pressure scoring. This is by design, not a defect.
