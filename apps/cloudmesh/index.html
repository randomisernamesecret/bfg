Cloudmesh

Cloudmesh tells you when rain will start and stop where you are, down to the minute. Its own forecast blends 39 weather sources and shows how confident it is, with live radar, air quality and an Apple Watch app alongside. Free to start, one-time Premium, no subscription.

See the weather, not just read it

Live radar, the next hour of rain, a ten-day forecast and cards for the things you do, in dark and light mode. Laid out so you can glance, decide, and get on with your day.

Cloud Buddy on your wrist

The Apple Watch app answers the one question that matters, at a glance: is it going to rain on me? You get the temperature, a one-line rain call and a little buddy who acts out the weather.

Rain at a glance

“No rain expected”, “Rain in 12 min” or “Rain stops in ~24 min”, for exactly where you are.

Dressed for the weather

Sunglasses in a heatwave, an umbrella before a shower, a bobble hat in the snow and a nightcap after dark. Tap your buddy to play and fetch a fresh forecast.

Pick a buddy on iPhone

Cloud Buddy is free. Cloud Kitty, Drip and Mochi come with Premium. Choose one in the iPhone app and it shows up on your watch.

Complications

Cloud Buddy, Current Weather, Umbrella and Rain Outlook put the forecast straight on your watch face.

What it does

Everything you need to plan around the weather, from minute rain alerts to live radar and air quality, without an account or a monthly fee.

Minute-by-minute rain

See when rain will start and stop at your exact location, and get a notification before it begins. Set quiet hours and choose alerts per city.

Live radar

A radar layer that follows the city you're looking at, with a precipitation overlay you can switch on and off.

A forecast that shows its confidence

Cloudmesh blends 39 sources: radar, observation networks and several forecast models. A confidence ribbon shows when the models agree, and when they don't.

Air quality & UV

An AQI now-cast, the UV index and a pollen-risk card in one glance, so you know whether it's a good day to be outside.

Apple Watch

Temperature, whether rain is coming and when it stops, with an animated Cloud Buddy that dresses for the weather. Complications put the next rain window on your watch face.

Widgets & activity cards

24 Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, plus about 30 cards for running, cycling, sailing, stargazing, drone flying and more.

Built around action, not data dumps

Most weather apps bury the one thing you actually care about: is it going to rain on me? Cloudmesh leads with the next hour of rain, live radar and timely alerts, so the answer is always one glance away.

  • Rain start and stop times for your exact location.
  • Alerts before the first drop, with quiet hours and per-city control.
  • A route planner that scores the weather along your way and can suggest a detour around rain (Premium).
  • Air quality and UV alongside the forecast, not hidden away.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cloudmesh have live radar?

Yes. Live rain radar follows your selected city so you can watch rain approach. Premium adds a minute-by-minute chart of the next hour.

Is Cloudmesh a subscription?

No. It's free to download with a generous free tier and a 7-day trial. Premium is a one-time purchase, with no recurring fee.
