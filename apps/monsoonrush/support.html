Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of game is Monsoon Rush?

Monsoon Rush is an arcade street racing game across Bangladesh: neon Dhaka nights, the great river bridge, the Sylhet tea hills and the Cox's Bazar coast road, often with a monsoon storm rolling in. You pick your first car on a tight budget, then work up each city's ladder of races, drag races, drift events, takedowns, knockouts, cups and storm runs. Upgrade your car part by part, customise its looks, and win the crew bosses' own cars in pink-slip duels.

How do I drive?

Swipe left or right to change line and tap NITRO for a burst. Tap NITRO again just as a burst ends for a stronger Perfect Nitro. Your nitro bar refills as you race, and faster with near misses, takedowns, barrel rolls off ramps and the glowing nitro canisters on the road. In drag races you shift gears yourself, and in drift events you hold DRIFT through the bends.

What happens if I hit traffic?

With nitro on you smash straight through. Without nitro, clipping a car bounces you back into your line and a square hit costs speed, but slamming into the back of a bus or truck will wreck you, so watch the tail lights.

How do pink slips work?

Each region's crew boss races you one on one. You stake one of your cars, never your last one. Win and their car is yours; lose and you can buy your car back or race them again. Pink slips are against the game's crews only, never other players.

Which devices are supported?

Monsoon Rush is made for iPhone and iPad.

Is Monsoon Rush free?

Yes. Monsoon Rush is free to play, with no ads. Optional in-app purchases are a coin pack, a permanent double-coins upgrade and two cosmetic packs. Cosmetics never change how a car performs, and everything else can be earned by racing.

How do I restore purchases?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with and use Restore Purchases in the game's Shop. The double-coins upgrade and cosmetic packs are permanent and can be restored. Coin packs are consumable: once coins are added to your game they are part of your local progress and cannot be restored separately.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Monsoon Rush has no accounts and doesn't collect any data. Deleting the game deletes its local progress, subject to your iOS and iCloud backup settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a bug?

Email blueflamegames@proton.me with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, what happened, and the steps to make it happen again. Please don't send passwords or payment details.
