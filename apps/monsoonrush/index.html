Nitro Metro

Race the world. Start in Hong Kong and Macau as the typhoon signal goes up, then take the World Tour: ten cities and 77 Grand Prix circuits. Swipe to change lanes, hit the ramps, fire the nitro and outrun the police.

Ten cities. 77 circuits.

Start in Hong Kong and Macau, then take the World Tour across ten world cities, from Paris and Tokyo to Las Vegas and Dubai, plus 77 Grand Prix circuits from racing history, each set in its own landscape. Famous skylines and landmarks line the streets, with two-way city traffic, highways with on-ramps, trams and long trucks.

Swipe. Jump. Nitro.

Swipe to change lanes, hit the ramps, fire the nitro and outrun the police. Easy to pick up, with plenty to master at 300 km/h.

Swipe to change lanes

Swipe left or right to slip through two-way city traffic, line up a ramp or pull alongside a rival.

Hit the ramps

Highways with on-ramps, jumps and long trucks to weave around, all at speed.

Fire the nitro

Light the nitro to blast past the pack, and break out of a police box-in.

Pick your season

Spring blossom, summer heat or golden autumn: choose the season and the route before each race.

Race the crews

Street races, circuits, cups and more. Win every way there is to win.

  • Street races, circuits, cups, knockouts, drift events and drag races with manual gears.
  • Takedowns: slam rivals into the barriers before the finish.
  • Pink slips: stake one of your cars and win the boss crew's ride.
  • The Signal 8 Run: Kowloon to Macau before the typhoon signal goes up, against the four boss crews.
  • Grand Prix circuits: 77 circuits from racing history, set in forest hills, harbours, deserts and mountains.

Escape the police

Police chases in every city, each with its own police cars, heat levels and roadblocks.

  • Boxed in? Break out with nitro.
  • Or take one of them down.
  • Watch for roadblocks as the heat level climbs.

Power-ups change everything

Rainbow mystery canisters hold oil slicks, roll cages, juggernauts and storm strikes. The back of the pack gets the strong ones.

  • Switch power-ups off in Settings any time.
  • Storms roll in mid-race: rain, lightning and wind through the grass. Halloween and Christmas dress the courses in their season.

Build your ride

16 cars, from a starter hatch to a six-wheeled prototype. Every car and upgrade is earned by racing.

  • Upgrade part by part: engine, turbo, nitrous, transmission, tires, suspension, brakes and weight.
  • Make it yours with paint, finishes, wheels, spoilers, window tint, vinyls, lamps and exhausts.
  • Add neon underglow for the night streets.

Around the world

Ten cities across four continents, with three radio stations plus shuffle for the road.

Europe Asia-Pacific Americas Middle East & Africa

Free to play. Fair to play.

Nitro Metro is free to play, and every car and upgrade is earned by racing. For players who want them, there are optional one-time purchases.

Coin Stack

A top-up of coins for when you want that next part now.

Double Coins

A one-time upgrade that permanently doubles the coins you earn.

Neon Underglow and Premium Paint

Style packs that change how a car looks, never how it performs.

The four World Tour continents

Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Americas, and the Middle East & Africa. Each can also be unlocked with coins earned by racing.

Frequently asked questions

When can I play Nitro Metro?

Nitro Metro is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. We'll link it here as soon as it's live.

Is Nitro Metro free?

Yes, it's free to play. The optional one-time purchases are Coin Stack, Double Coins, Neon Underglow, Premium Paint Finishes and the four World Tour continents. Every continent can also be unlocked with coins earned by racing, and every car and upgrade is earned by racing.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, no tracking and no account.

How do I drive?

Swipe left or right to change lanes and fire the nitro to break away. In drag races you shift gears yourself.

How do pink slips work?

You stake one of your cars against a boss crew. Win and you take their ride.

Does it play offline?

Yes. Nitro Metro plays offline, with no account to sign in to.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Nitro Metro doesn't collect any data, and purchases are handled by Apple.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.
