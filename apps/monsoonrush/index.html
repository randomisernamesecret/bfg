Monsoon Rush

The monsoon is coming. Race it. Street racing across Bangladesh, from neon Dhaka nights to the coast road at Cox's Bazar: swipe to change lanes, hit the ramps, fire the nitro and outrun the storm.

Bangladesh at full throttle

Four regions, each with its own ladder of events and a boss crew at the top: the neon nights of Dhaka, the mega bridge over the Padma, the misty tea hills of Sylhet and the longest beach road in the world at Cox's Bazar. And somewhere behind you, the monsoon is always coming.

Swipe. Jump. Nitro.

Swipe to change lanes, hit the ramps, fire the nitro and outrun the storm. Easy to pick up, with plenty to master at 350 km/h.

Swipe to change lanes

Swipe left or right to slip through traffic, line up a ramp or pull alongside a rival. With nitro lit, you smash straight through the cars in your way.

Hit the ramps

Ramps, trick jumps and billboard smashes. Swipe in the air to nail the landing for a boost.

Fill the nitro

Near misses, slipstreams and takedowns all fill your nitro bar, and so do the glowing canisters on the road.

PERFECT NITRO

Tap NITRO again just as a burst ends and you get a stronger PERFECT NITRO. Timing is everything.

Race the crews

Eight crews run these roads. Beat them at their own events, then take their cars.

  • Street races, three-lap circuits, cups, knockouts, drift events and drag races with manual gears.
  • Takedown events: slam rivals into the barriers before the finish.
  • Outrun duels: get 300 m clear and the win is yours.
  • Pink slips: stake one of your cars and win the boss crew's ride.
  • The Monsoon Run: Dhaka to Cox's Bazar in one go, against all four boss crews.

Escape the police

Heat stars climb, roadblocks go up and the patrol cars start working as a team.

  • Get boxed in and they squeeze you slower and slower until you're busted.
  • Break out with nitro, or shove one of them into the wall.
  • A takedown refills your nitro, and every cop you take down pays.

Ride the storm

The monsoon doesn't wait for the start line. It rolls in mid-race, and suddenly you're racing on flooded roads through lightning and lashing rain.

  • Storm runs: the monsoon front is right behind you. Reach the finish before it swallows you.
  • Fit street, rain or slick tyres for the weather ahead.

Build your ride

32 cars, from a humble starter hatch to a six-wheeled flagship. Every one of them can be earned by racing.

  • Upgrade part by part: engine, turbo, nitrous, transmission, tyres, suspension, brakes and weight reduction.
  • Make it yours with paint and accent colours, wheels, spoilers, window tint, vinyls, lamps and exhausts.
  • Win the boss crews' own cars in pink-slip duels.

Turn it up

Three radio stations plus shuffle, for whatever the road ahead calls for.

Race anthems Synthwave after dark Street fusion Shuffle

Free to play. Fair to play.

Monsoon Rush is free to play, and every car and every upgrade can be earned by racing. For players who want them, there are four optional extras.

Coin Stack

A top-up of coins for when you want that next part now. Everything it buys can also be earned on the road.

Double Coins

A one-time upgrade that permanently doubles the coins you earn.

Neon Underglow

A style pack that lights up the road beneath your car. Purely cosmetic: it never changes how a car performs.

Premium Paint Finishes

A style pack of extra paint finishes. Just as cosmetic: it makes you look fast, not go faster.

Frequently asked questions

When can I play Monsoon Rush?

Monsoon Rush is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. We'll link it here as soon as it's live.

Is Monsoon Rush free?

Yes, it's free to play. The optional extras are a Coin Stack, a permanent Double Coins upgrade and two cosmetic style packs, Neon Underglow and Premium Paint Finishes. Every car and every upgrade can be earned by racing.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, no tracking and no account.

How do I drive?

Swipe left or right to change lanes and tap NITRO for a burst. Tap it again just as a burst ends for a PERFECT NITRO. In drag races you shift gears yourself, and in drift events you hold DRIFT through the bends.

How do pink slips work?

Each region's boss crew races you one on one. You stake one of your cars, never your last one. Win and their car is yours. Lose and you can buy yours back or race them again. Pink slips are against the game's crews only, never other players.

Does it play offline?

Yes. Monsoon Rush plays offline, with no account to sign in to.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Monsoon Rush doesn't collect any data, and purchases are handled by Apple.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.
