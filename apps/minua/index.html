How Minua works

Choose who Minua is for, pick the apps you want more of and the apps you want less of, then set the ratio and the hours. The balance shows minutes earned, minutes used and what's left. When it runs out, the fun apps lock again, and each morning starts fresh.

What it does

One set of rules, whether Minua is on your child's iPhone or iPad or on your own phone. You pick the apps, the ratio and the hours.

Choose who it's for

At setup, pick My child or Myself. The rules work the same way; the wording and the safeguards fit who's using it.

Earning apps

Reading, math, languages, workouts, meditation: whatever you want more of. On a child's device, these are the learning apps you choose.

Fun apps

Social media, videos, games. They stay locked until there's time in the balance, and lock again when it runs out.

Your ratio

Anywhere from 1:1 to 12:1. At 6:1, six minutes of earning unlocks one minute of fun.

Allowed hours and a daily cap

Set the hours when fun apps can unlock, plus a daily earning cap. Outside allowed hours, fun apps stay locked.

Fresh every morning

The balance resets every day, so each morning starts with a clean slate.

On your child's device

Set the rules once, then hand over the iPhone or iPad. Your child sees exactly how much time they've earned and what unlocks more: no surveillance, no nagging, nothing sent anywhere.

  • Settings and purchases sit behind a 4-digit parent PIN, with a timed lockout after repeated wrong attempts.
  • On a child's device in Family Sharing, you approve Minua with your Apple Account, so your child can't switch the permission off or delete Minua without you.
  • Setup tips to prevent app deletion and idle earning.

For yourself

For adults breaking their own scroll habit. Put social media, videos and games behind reading, languages, workouts or meditation, and let the scroll wait until you've earned it.

  • Put the apps you scroll behind the apps you want to spend more time in.
  • Wording written for adults, and a PIN only if you want one.
  • Your phone, your rules: you can change them in Settings whenever you decide to. Minua just makes the scroll wait until you've earned it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Minua only for kids?

No. At setup you choose My child or Myself. Parents use it on a child's iPhone or iPad; adults use it on their own phone to break the scroll habit.

Does Minua spy on anyone?

No. Minua has no accounts, no servers and no tracking. Your settings and your balance stay on the device.

How does earning work?

Time in the apps you want more of unlocks time in the apps you want less of, at a ratio you set from 1:1 to 12:1.

Can my child switch Minua off?

On a child's device in Family Sharing, you approve Minua with your Apple Account, so your child can't switch the permission off or delete Minua without you. Settings and purchases sit behind a parent PIN.

Can I change my own rules?

Yes. In Myself mode it's your phone and your rules: you can change them in Settings whenever you decide to. The PIN is optional.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account and no sign-up. Every feature is free for 7 days; after that, a single one-time purchase unlocks Minua for good. No subscription.
