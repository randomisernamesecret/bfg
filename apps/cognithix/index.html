What it does

Four subjects, short daily practice and a clear picture of progress, all in one app.

English

Vocabulary, spelling, grammar, punctuation, comprehension and cloze passages, with hints and an explanation for every answer.

Verbal reasoning

Letter and number series, analogies, word codes, hidden words, compound words, logic and more: 11 topics in all.

Non-verbal reasoning

Shape series, matrices, rotations, reflections, odd one out, cube nets, fold-and-punch and codes: 8 topics in all.

Language aptitude

Sound clues, word matching, rhymes, number learning and sentence building, plus bonus languages such as Latin and Esperanto.

Daily plans and mock tests

A short daily plan suggests what to practise next and varies the topics. Difficulty adapts during practice, and timed full papers, half papers and speed rounds build exam stamina.

A pet worth practising for

Lessons earn coins to feed, wash and dress a virtual pet. Stars, badges and streaks make regular practice feel rewarding.

Know exactly where to help

It's hard to tell whether revision is working. The parent view shows how each child is doing: time spent this week, sessions, accuracy, and which topics are mastered, secure or due for review. Struggle insights point out patterns such as low accuracy, repeated mistakes, rushed answers or a falling trend.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cover maths?

No. Cognithix covers English, verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning and language aptitude. If your child's test includes maths, add a maths resource alongside it.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Profiles and progress are stored only on the device, behind a Parent PIN with a recovery code. Progress doesn't sync between devices.

How does pricing work?

Everything is free to try for 7 days. After that, one in-app purchase unlocks the full app for every child profile, with no subscription and no ads. Read Cognithix vs Atom Learning for a side-by-side look.
