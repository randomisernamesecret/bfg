What it does

One creative studio for coloring, drawing, animating, storytelling and music — built so everyone always ends a session with something they made.

Color In

Kids color by hand inside the lines, pinch to zoom in on small details, and “Magic edges” helps the little ones. Neat-coloring stars build steady hands — and it never scolds.

Color by Number

Simple pages for kids, intricate relaxing pictures and mandalas for grown-ups. Tap the numbered areas with the matching color and watch the picture appear.

Frequently asked questions

Is it just for kids?

No. Kids draw, animate and make stories; grown-ups get their own profile with a calm corner for coloring and color by number.

How does the coloring work?

In Color In, kids color by hand inside the lines, with pinch-to-zoom for small details and stars for neat coloring. In Color by Number, you tap the numbered areas with the matching color and the picture appears. There are 146 pages in all.

Is it safe for young children?

Yes. There's no social sharing, no public gallery and no stranger interaction — a parental gate keeps purchases, sharing and settings out of little hands.

Does it need an account or the internet?

No. There's no sign-up, it works offline, and everything anyone creates stays on the device; grown-ups decide what gets shared. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and the app is in English for now.

What's the story helper?

Creating is the point — coloring, drawing, animating, storybooks and music come first. On supported devices, the unlock also adds an optional story helper: Sparky takes turns with a child on a story they started, and every reply is checked before a child sees it.
