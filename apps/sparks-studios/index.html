Draw it, fill it, watch it dance

Draw and fill, animate, color, build storybooks and make music — for kids and grown-ups. Everything anyone makes stays on the device.

What it does

Drawing and filling come first, then animating, coloring, storytelling and music — built so everyone always ends a session with something they made.

Draw it, fill it

Sketch any shape, then tap inside it to fill it with color. Only that shape fills, even if a line doesn’t quite meet.

Watch it dance

Bring the character you drew to life — it can walk, run, dance, wave, jump, sit or fall asleep.

Color In

Kids color by hand inside the lines, pinch to zoom in on small details, and “Magic edges” helps the little ones. Neat-coloring stars build steady hands — and it never scolds.

Color by Number

Simple pages for kids, intricate relaxing pictures and mandalas for grown-ups. Tap the numbered areas with the matching color — finish the picture and the original painting is revealed exactly, shading and all.

Grown-up profiles

Grown-ups get their own profile, with a calm corner that opens on coloring and no math challenges.

Storybooks

Stories become illustrated storybooks starring a child's own drawings, with a soundtrack. "Read to me" reads every page aloud.

Music maker

Loops, beats and instruments — piano, drums, strings — plus rain, birds and the sea, with ready-made tunes to start from.

Drawing tools

Brushes, shapes, stickers, text and tap-to-fill, with undo, pinch-to-zoom and pan. Sized for small hands; works with Apple Pencil.

Gallery of keepsakes

Every creation is saved with a picture thumbnail. Brothers and sisters switch profiles with "Who's creating?"

Calm, safe screen time

No ads, no child accounts, no analytics. Daily time limits with "+15 minutes today", and a grown-ups-only area behind a parental gate.

Frequently asked questions

Is it just for kids?

No. Kids draw, fill, animate and make stories; grown-ups get their own profile with a calm corner for coloring and color by number, and no math challenges.

How does the coloring work?

In Color In, kids color by hand inside the lines, with pinch-to-zoom for small details and stars for neat coloring. In Color by Number, you tap the numbered areas with the matching color, and finishing the picture reveals the original painting exactly, shading and all. There are 146 pages in all. On your own drawings, tap inside any shape to fill it.

Is it safe for young children?

Yes. There's no social sharing, no public gallery and no stranger interaction — a parental gate keeps purchases, sharing and settings out of little hands.

Does it need an account or the internet?

No. There's no sign-up, it works offline, and everything anyone creates stays on the device; grown-ups decide what gets shared. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and the app is in English for now.

What's the story helper?

Creating is the point — drawing, filling, animating, coloring, storybooks and music come first. On supported devices, the unlock also adds an optional story helper: Sparky takes turns with a child on a story they started, and every reply is checked before a child sees it.
