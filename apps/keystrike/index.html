See it in action

A clear path through every practice session: play a piece at your own tempo, read real engraved sheet music, and let the home screen point you to the next step. On iPad, iPhone and Mac.

What's inside

A complete practice room — graded lessons, falling-note pieces, theory woven into the steps, and tools to bring your own music.

Guided grade journey

Move from Grade 0 to Grade 8 at your own pace. Each grade ends with an exam-style check, and a daily plan keeps the next useful step in view.

Sheet music or falling notes

Read engraved sheet music or follow falling notes. Slow the tempo, practise hands separately and loop the hard sections.

Theory inside practice

Staff reading, rhythm, intervals, scales, chords, dynamics, and articulation are introduced through lesson steps and reference material.

Sight-reading & ear training

Treble and bass clef reading drills, ear-copy challenges, and short checks move practice beyond memorising songs.

Scales, chords & improv

Scale tests, chord drills and improvisation steps build technique, and free play lets you record your own ideas and export them as MIDI.

Built-in pieces

27 public-domain pieces, from Bach, Mozart and Beethoven to Chopin, Debussy, Satie and Joplin, ready to play from the start.

Bring your own MIDI

Import standard .mid or .midi files from Files and Keystrike turns them into playable sheet music you can practise like any built-in piece.

Acoustic-piano mode

No MIDI keyboard? Acoustic mode uses the microphone: it hears single notes in lessons and records whole pieces to grade them on your device after you finish. MIDI is best for chords and two hands.

Reports & coach loop

Track completed lessons, retained skills and practice time. Weak spots turn into short, targeted drills, and a progress report helps a teacher or parent follow along.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a MIDI keyboard?

No. Start with the on-screen piano, use a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard for the most precise input, or play a real piano in acoustic mode through the microphone.

How many grades are there?

A Grade 0 foundation, then Grades 1 to 8, each ending with an exam-style check. The grades are Keystrike's own progression, not an external exam qualification.

Is there an Android version?

No. Keystrike is built for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Will my progress sync between devices?

No. Keystrike is local-first, so progress, recordings and imported MIDI files stay on the device they were created on. You can back them up and export them.

Can I use my own sheet music?

You can't import PDFs. You can import MIDI files (.mid, .midi), and Keystrike turns them into playable sheet music automatically.

Is it for children or adults?

Both. The early grades are gentle enough for a focused eight-year-old; the later grades go deep enough for adults tackling advanced repertoire.
