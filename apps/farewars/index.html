Fare Wars

Welcome to Rivergate

A fictional river city bursting with colour, inspired by South Asian streets and hand-painted buses. Market stalls, ferry landings, a busy station, the college crowd and the lanes of Old Town: every district has riders waiting, and every one of them is up for grabs.

One thumb. The whole city.

Fare Wars is built to be played one-handed. Every shift is a sprint: pick up, drop off, keep the clock alive.

Swipe to change lanes

Swipe left or right to switch lanes and cut in ahead of a rival bus before it reaches the waiting riders.

Pick your road

At every junction, swipe toward the district your riders want: Market, Ferry Ghat, Station, College or Old Town. The right turn is the fast turn.

Pull in, they hop on

Pull down to pull into a stop. Riders board by themselves, so your thumb stays on the race.

Beat the shift clock

Every delivery adds time to the clock. Fast, smooth driving earns tips and speed bonuses. Drive like a maniac and your riders bail out. It's slapstick, never gory.

Every fare is a fight

You're not the only bus in town. Named rival crews race you to the same waiting passengers, and they don't queue politely.

Rival crews

Named crews chase the same riders you do. Get to the stop first, or watch your fare ride off in someone else's bus.

Boss races

Every district has a boss. Beat them head-to-head in a boss race to win the next route permit.

Five route permits

Five permits in all, each opening a new route with bigger fares. Rivergate gets bigger, and so does your pay.

From rust bucket to river legend

You start with the Cricket, a battered little minibus with more character than horsepower. From there, you earn your way to a garage of restored legends.

The Cricket

Small, scrappy and held together by optimism. Your first bus, and your first fares.

The Weaver

A restored midi bus: a real step up in size, and a lot more presence at the stop.

The Atlas

The full-size legend. When the Atlas pulls in, the whole street turns to look.

Six upgrade tracks on every bus, so each one in your garage can be tuned to the way you drive:

The paint shop

In Rivergate, a bus is a canvas. Make yours the one people point at.

  • Paint the body, stripe, roof and trim separately.
  • Choose gloss, satin, metallic or pearl finishes.
  • Place folk-art decals anywhere on the bus: lotus, peacock, swallow, tiger, river boat and more.
  • Add roof racks, luggage, tassel mudflaps and rabbit-ear mirrors.

Free to play. Fair to play.

Fare Wars is free to play, and everything in it can be earned on the road. For players who want them, there are a few optional extras.

Optional coin pack

A top-up for when you want that new paint job now, not after the next shift.

Double fares, forever

A one-time upgrade that permanently doubles the fares you earn.

Two cosmetic packs

Extra looks for your buses. Purely cosmetic: they make you look good, not drive faster.

Frequently asked questions

When can I play Fare Wars?

Fare Wars is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. We'll link it here as soon as it's live.

Is Fare Wars free?

Yes, it's free to play. There's an optional coin pack, a permanent double-fares upgrade and two cosmetic packs, and everything can be earned by playing.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, no tracking and no account.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Fare Wars doesn't collect any data, and purchases are handled by Apple.

Is it violent?

Not really. Drive recklessly and your riders bail out, but it's played for laughs: slapstick, no gore.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.
