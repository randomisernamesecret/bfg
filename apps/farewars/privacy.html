Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Fare Wars is an arcade bus racing game for iPhone and iPad by Blue Flame Games. The short version: Fare Wars does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data We Collect

None. Blue Flame Games does not collect, store, transmit or receive any personal information or usage data from Fare Wars.

Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following locally on your iPhone or iPad. It never leaves your device and we never see it:

  • Game progress, such as route permits unlocked
  • Coins, your bus garage, upgrades and paint shop designs
  • Your in-game settings
  • Which optional purchases are unlocked

Purchases

Fare Wars is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (a coin pack, a permanent double-fares upgrade and two cosmetic packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment card information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those transactions.

What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising of any kind
  • No analytics services
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No tracking, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
  • No accounts, sign-in or player profiles
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Apple Services

Apple may process App Store activity and device diagnostics under your own device settings and Apple's privacy policy. That data is handled by Apple, not by Fare Wars.

Children

Because Fare Wars collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting Fare Wars removes its locally stored game data from your device, subject to your normal iOS and iCloud backup settings.

Emailing Us

If you contact us for support, we receive only what you choose to include in your email and use it to reply. Please don't send passwords or payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
