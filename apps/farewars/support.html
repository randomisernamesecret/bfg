Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of game is Fare Wars?

Fare Wars is an arcade bus racing game set in Rivergate, a fictional, colourful river city. You race rival crews to waiting passengers, deliver them to the right district, beat each district's boss to earn new route permits, and build up a garage of restored buses.

How do I drive?

Everything works with one thumb. Swipe left or right to change lanes. At junctions, swipe toward the district your riders want. Pull down to pull into a stop; riders board automatically.

How does the shift clock work?

Every delivery adds time to your shift clock. Fast, smooth deliveries earn tips and speed bonuses. Reckless driving makes riders bail out, so a steady hand pays.

How do I unlock new routes?

Beat a district's boss in a boss race to win the next route permit. There are five permits in all, and each route pays bigger fares.

Which devices are supported?

Fare Wars is made for iPhone and iPad.

Is Fare Wars free?

Yes. Fare Wars is free to play, with no ads. Optional in-app purchases are a coin pack, a permanent double-fares upgrade and two cosmetic packs. Everything can also be earned by playing.

How do I restore purchases?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with and use Restore Purchases in the game. The double-fares upgrade and cosmetic packs are permanent and can be restored. Coin packs are consumable: once coins are added to your game they are part of your local progress and cannot be restored separately.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Fare Wars has no accounts and doesn't collect any data. Deleting the game deletes its local progress, subject to your iOS and iCloud backup settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a bug?

Email blueflamegames@proton.me with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, what happened, and the steps to make it happen again. Please don't send passwords or payment details.
