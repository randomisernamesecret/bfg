Hollowstead

Seven years after the Blight, you inherit a farm in a quiet valley. The soil is still good. The dead don't stay down. Grow food through four seasons, make friends in the fort and the salt town, and keep your fences standing when the Hollowed come calling.

A year on the farm

Every day starts in the fields and ends at the fence line. In between, the valley is yours.

Grow through four seasons

Plant, water and harvest crops that change with spring, summer, autumn and winter. A good harvest feeds you and fills your pockets.

Make friends

The fort and the salt town are full of people trying to start again. Talk, help and trade, and they'll start to count on you.

Trade and cook

Sell what you grow, buy what you can't, and cook proper meals to keep you going through the long days.

Fish and keep animals

Cast a line at the water, raise animals on the farm, and slowly make the old place feel like home.

When the light goes

The Hollowed are the dead who got up again. By night they test your fences, and later in the year they stop waiting for dark.

Build defences

Put up fences and walls where they come through, and mend what they break before the next visit.

Set traps

Lay traps along the paths they take, so fewer of them ever reach the house.

Light lanterns

Light pushes the dark back. Keep the lanterns burning and you'll see them coming.

The Callow Deep

Under the old town lies a mine with no bottom anyone has found. It's never the same twice.

New floors every visit

The Deep shifts between trips, so every descent is a new one.

Better ore the deeper you go

The further down you dare, the richer the ore for your tools and your defences.

A quick way home

Every fifth floor has a way back up, so next time you can start deeper.

Your first year, your story

The story runs across your first year in five chapters, and the choices you make change who stands with you when it counts. Or play Casual: no story, just the farm and the people.

Casual

No story. Just the farm, the seasons and the people of the valley.

Survival

The full story, with the Hollowed at your fences. The way Hollowstead is meant to be played.

Hardcore

Survival with real stakes. One fall can cost you the farm.

Frequently asked questions

When can I play Hollowstead?

Hollowstead is in development and coming to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. We'll link it here as soon as it's live.

Is Hollowstead free?

Chapter 1 is free to play, with no ads. A single one-time purchase unlocks the full game. There is no subscription.

Who are the Hollowed?

The dead who got up again after the Blight. They test your fences at night, and later by day too. Defences, traps and lanterns keep them out.

Can I just farm?

Yes. Casual has no story: just the farm, the seasons and the people. Survival and Hardcore bring the full story and the Hollowed.

What is the Callow Deep?

An endless mine under the old town, with new floors on every visit and better ore the deeper you go. Every fifth floor has a quick way home.

Are there ads or tracking?

No. There are no ads and no tracking, and the whole game runs on your device.
