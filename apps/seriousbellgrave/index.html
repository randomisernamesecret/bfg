Serious Bellgrave · First-person survival action

Hold the line. Save Bellgrave.

The dead have taken the city. Keep its streets open while families escape to sanctuary. Nine weapons, three districts and boss battles that change as the fight escalates.

Every district changes the fight

Take on the wardens

Read the Bellkeeper’s pulses, dodge the Cinder Marshal’s slams and survive the Glass Widow’s attacks. Their second phases demand new tactics.

Build your hunter

Level up, earn points and choose how to develop your Gunslinger, Warden and Occultist skills. Training carries between runs.

Fight your way through

Pick up weapons and ammunition, destroy cover, dash through danger and keep moving while you reload.

Your progress stays with you

The game stores character progress and settings on your device. Gameplay works offline. The optional More Games menu opens Apple’s App Store when you select another app.

Game menus and text are currently in English.

Try the Courtyard. Unlock the whole fight.

The free demo takes you through the Courtyard and its first boss. Keep your earned training, then open all districts and endless runs with one permanent full-game purchase.
