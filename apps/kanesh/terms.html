4. Data Accuracy and Timing

The data in Tickview comes from third parties. It may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, revised later, or unavailable. Prices are not real-time quotes and must not be used to place or time trades. Currency conversions are indicative. Always check the original source, which Tickview links to, before relying on any figure.

Widgets, answers given through Siri and Shortcuts, and cards you share show a figure as it was when Tickview last fetched it, which may be some time ago. Each carries the time or date of its data. A widget cannot fetch data itself and can lag behind the app.

5. Holdings, Gain and Loss

If you enter share counts and costs, Tickview shows values, day changes, gains and losses for those positions. These figures are estimates. They are calculated on your device from third-party prices and exchange rates, which may be delayed or wrong, and from the numbers you typed, which Tickview cannot check. A gain or loss shown in your display currency is the gain or loss in the listing's currency converted at the latest rate; it does not include currency movements since your purchase, and it takes no account of dividends, of fees you left out of the cost, or of tax. A position with a missing cost, price or rate is left out of a total, and the total says so.

These figures are not a statement of any account and are not figures for a tax return. Your broker's or provider's records are the record. When Tickview describes your watchlist or holdings it describes the data you entered; it is not commenting on your positions or proposing a change to them.

6. Model Estimates and Statistics

Some screens (for example Recession Watch) show the output of published statistical models and historical track records, calculated by Tickview from public data. These are estimates, not predictions or forecasts. Models can be wrong: they have given false alarms and missed events in the past, and a historical track record describes the past, not the future. These calculations are made by Tickview and are not produced, reviewed or endorsed by the institutions that published the models or the data.

7. Alerts and Notifications

Alerts are a convenience. They may arrive late, arrive more than once, or not arrive at all, because of device settings, background limits set by the operating system, connectivity or data-provider outages. Do not rely on Tickview alerts for trading, risk management or any time-sensitive decision.

8. AI-Generated Text

Short summaries in Tickview may be generated automatically by an AI model running on your device. Generated text can be inaccurate or incomplete and is not advice. The figures on the card and the linked source take precedence over any summary.

In Tickview 1.2.0 and later, all AI runs on your device; Blue Flame Games does not access, collect or store your questions, and they are not sent to any AI service. Earlier versions (1.1.x) had an optional bring-your-own-key mode that could send the data described in the Privacy Policy to a provider of your choice under your own account and its terms; that mode has been removed.

9. Cards You Share

Tickview can turn a result into an image for you to share. A card carries public figures with the name of their source, the date of the data and the source's link. Tickview never puts your share counts, position values, costs or notes on a card; a card made from your brief or from the schedule does name the tickers it covers. Sharing a card is your own act: you decide whether to share it and with whom, and you are responsible for how you use it. A card is information only, like everything else in Tickview. It is not advice and not an invitation to anyone to buy or sell an investment, and it shows data as last fetched.
