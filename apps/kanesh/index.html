Your watchlist, with the sources attached

Follow the companies you care about, read the chart and open the source behind a result. Type a question in plain English, such as “Apple stock price” or “Bitcoin price”.

What it does

A research companion for the stocks you follow: watchlist, charts, filings, headlines and economic data, with the source in view so you can check what you read.

Watchlists & charts

Follow stocks, ETFs, funds, cryptocurrencies and commodities. The free app holds five; Kanesh Pro removes the limit and adds position sizes.

Sources you can open

Result cards name where their data came from, such as Yahoo Finance, SEC EDGAR or FRED, and link to it. Check the original and its date before you rely on a number.

Company filings

See a company's recent SEC filings, from 10-K and 10-Q reports to 8-Ks and insiders' Form 4s, and open each one on EDGAR.

Plain-English questions

Ask for a price, a chart or a comparison in your own words. Local AI answers on supported devices; other devices use built-in query routing and factual summaries.

Headlines & the economy

Headlines open at the publisher. The markets view shows indices, sectors, funds, commodities and currencies, and a free FRED key adds rates, inflation, jobs and housing data.

Kanesh Pro

An optional upgrade: unlimited watchlist with position sizes, portfolio exposure, company comparisons, earnings prep, a morning brief, investment theses and a decision journal.

Private, not just promised

Kanesh has no account and no analytics. Your watchlist, position sizes, theses and journal are stored on your device, and Blue Flame Games never receives them.

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads.
  • Data requests carry only what the data needs: a ticker, a series or a currency code.
  • Any free data-provider keys you add are stored on your device and used only with that provider.
  • Local AI works on the device, on supported devices.

Who it's for

Kanesh is for individual investors, students and curious people who follow a handful of companies and want to check what they read against the source, on a phone. It fits the intent behind searches like stock research app for iPhone, SEC filings app and Bloomberg alternative for individual investors, as long as you want a research companion rather than a trading terminal.

Primary sources first

Frequently asked questions

Can I verify the data myself?

Yes, and you should. Result cards name their source and link to it, and filings open on SEC EDGAR. Kanesh presents financial information, not investment advice.

Is Kanesh only for US markets?

Quotes cover stocks, funds, crypto and commodities on many exchanges, but company filings come from SEC EDGAR, so they cover companies that file with the SEC. Check the currency and exchange before comparing prices across markets.

A Bloomberg alternative on my phone?

For individual research it covers useful ground: charts, filings, news and economic data with the sources in view. It is not a professional terminal and it does not place trades. The guides below compare the options.
