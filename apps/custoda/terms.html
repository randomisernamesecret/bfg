1. Important information

Custoda helps you keep track. It does not replace reading the label. Product information comes from shared databases and from labels you scan, and it can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Recipes change without the barcode changing.

If you or someone you cook for has an allergy, read the pack every time. Do not use Custoda as the only way to manage a severe allergy. If nothing on your list is found, that is not a guarantee: read the label. If product data is missing, Custoda cannot check it: read the label. If a recipe is filtered with an avoid list, it is filtered using recipe data, so check every ingredient you buy.

Dates are reminders, not a check that food is fit to eat. If food looks or smells wrong, do not eat it. For medicines, follow the date and instructions on the pack and ask a pharmacist if you are unsure. Reminders for smoke alarms and first-aid kits are a prompt to check, not a test of the device.

Custoda is a tracking and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical, dietary or pharmaceutical advice. You are responsible for your own health decisions.
