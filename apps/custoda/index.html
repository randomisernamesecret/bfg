Your pantry, remembered

Scan it, confirm the date, and let Custoda do the remembering. The screens below are the real app, exactly as it appears on the App Store.

What it does

One inventory for the kitchen, the medicine cabinet and the rest of the house, with reminders before things run out of date and recipes that use what you already have.

Scan, then confirm the date

Scan a barcode to identify a product, then read the printed date with the camera or tap a quick-pick: 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months or 1 year. Estimated dates are a starting point, and Custoda asks you to check them against the label.

A whole shop in one go

Scan item after item, then set every expiry date, rename products and mark how much is left on a single screen.

Avoid-list warnings

Add everyone's allergies once, with one tap from the common allergens or by typing your own. When you scan, Custoda warns you when it finds something from your avoid list in the information it has: “Contains X” or “May contain traces of X”. The warning stays on screen until you dismiss it, and VoiceOver reads it out. Diets such as vegan, gluten-free, halal or low sugar filter your recipes and show what the product data says about them. Your avoid list, its warnings and “Read the label” are free for everyone.

Nothing found? Read the label

When Custoda finds nothing from your avoid list in the information it has, it says “Read the label”, with a short reason line where there is one. It never says a product is all right.

Use it before it's gone

The home screen puts what's expired and what's expiring soon at the top, suggests recipes that use it, and keeps a running tally of food rescued and food wasted.

Cook from your shelf

818 recipes across nine regional cuisines, from Italian and Japanese to Romanian and American BBQ & Grill. "For you" picks are ranked by what's already on your shelf, "Explore by region" browses each cuisine, and every recipe opens on an illustrated banner. Recipes that name something from your avoid list are hidden, and a recipe with an ingredient that may hide one carries a note. Save favourites and add your own recipes.

How much is left

Set a pack size and how much is left: 25, 50 or 75 per cent, full, or fine-tuned. Recipes use it, so a nearly empty box doesn't count as enough. One swipe marks an item used or wasted, or puts it back on the shopping list.

Medicine, household and safety

Track medicines, toiletries and household supplies alongside food. Save replace-by dates for smoke alarms, carbon-monoxide detectors, fire extinguishers and first-aid kits.

Reminders that wait for you

Get a reminder before something expires. Mark it used from the notification, or snooze it for two days.

Share with your household

Invite family members with a private iCloud invitation and keep one shopping list together. You can also share selected food from your inventory, which is off until you turn it on.

Settle dinner with a vote

Suggest a few recipes, see how many ingredients each person already has, and let the household vote. Custoda shows the leading choice.

Your names, your language

Rename "Semi Skimmed Milk 2L" to "Milk": recipes go by the name you give an item, and treat minced and ground as the same thing. Light and dark themes follow your device, and the app speaks six languages.

Private, not just promised

Most household apps want an account before you can add a thing. Custoda starts working the moment you open it, and your avoid list and medicines stay on your device.

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Your avoid list, diets and medicines are not sent anywhere by Custoda and are not shared with your household (a backup of your device, if you make one, includes them).
  • Household lists, selected food and saved recipes sync through your own iCloud, only when you use them.
  • Barcode lookups send only the barcode number to public product databases.
  • No ads and no tracking.

Before you start

Custoda helps you keep track. It does not replace reading the label. Product information comes from shared databases and from labels you scan, and it can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Recipes change without the barcode changing. If you or someone you cook for has an allergy, read the pack every time. Never use Custoda as the only way to manage a severe allergy. Dates are reminders, not a safety check. If food looks or smells wrong, do not eat it. Custoda is not medical advice. Ingredient data may be incomplete: always read the product label and ask a pharmacist or doctor.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Custoda works without sign-up. Your shelf, reminders and avoid list live on your device. The optional household features use your own iCloud, and only when you turn them on.

What does Custoda say when it finds nothing?

“Read the label.” Custoda warns you when it finds something from your avoid list in the information it has, and it never says a product is all right. Read the pack every time, and never use Custoda as the only way to manage a severe allergy. Custoda is not medical advice.

How does household sharing work?

Invite family members with a private iCloud invitation. Everyone shares one shopping list and can suggest recipes and vote on dinner. Sharing food from your inventory is off by default: you choose which items to share and can stop at any time. Your allergen and diet profile is never shared.

Does it track medicines and safety equipment too?

Yes. Keep food and drink, medicines, toiletries and household supplies in one inventory, and save replace-by dates for smoke alarms, carbon-monoxide detectors, fire extinguishers and first-aid supplies.

What does it cost?

Try every feature for 7 days with up to 20 items. After the trial you can keep up to 5 items free, along with medicine and safety reminders, scanning, recipes and your whole avoid list with its warnings. A one-time purchase adds unlimited items, reminders for food and household items, filtering recipes by diet, and sharing with your household (shared list, shared pantry and dinner vote). There is no subscription.

Which devices and languages?

Custoda is built for iPhone and iPad. The app is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, with light and dark themes that follow your device.
