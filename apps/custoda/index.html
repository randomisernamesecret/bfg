Custoda

Your pantry, remembered

Scan it, confirm the date, and let Custoda do the remembering. The screens below are the real app, exactly as it appears on the App Store.

What it does

One inventory for the kitchen, the medicine cabinet and the rest of the house, with reminders before things run out of date and recipes that use what you already have.

Scan, then confirm the date

Scan a barcode to identify a product, then read the printed date with the camera or tap a quick-pick: three days, a week, a month, a year. Estimated dates are a starting point, and Custoda asks you to check them against the label.

A whole shop in one go

Scan item after item, then set every expiry date, rename products and mark how much is left on a single screen.

Allergen and diet warnings

Add household allergies with one tap from the common allergens, or type your own ingredients. Pick dietary choices such as vegan, gluten-free, halal or low sugar. Custoda flags matches as you scan, whenever product information is available.

Honest when it can't be sure

If ingredient data is missing or incomplete, Custoda shows "unverified". It never tells you a product is safe. Allergen warnings are never behind the paid unlock.

Use it before it's gone

The home screen puts what's expired and what's expiring soon at the top, suggests recipes that use it, and keeps a running tally of food rescued and food wasted.

Cook from your shelf

More than 750 recipes across nine cuisines, ranked by what you already have. Search by dish, ingredient or cuisine, save favourites, and add your own recipes.

How much is left

Mark each item full, three-quarters, half or a quarter left. Running low? Swipe a finished item and it goes straight onto the shopping list.

Medicine, household and safety

Track medicines, toiletries and household supplies alongside food. Save replace-by dates for smoke alarms, carbon-monoxide detectors, fire extinguishers and first-aid kits.

Reminders that wait for you

Get a reminder before something expires. Mark it used from the notification, or snooze it for two days.

Share with your household

Invite family members with a private iCloud invitation and keep one shopping list together. You can also share selected food from your inventory, which is off until you turn it on.

Settle dinner with a vote

Suggest a few recipes, see how many ingredients each person already has, and let the household vote. Custoda shows the leading choice.

Your names, your language

Rename "Semi Skimmed Milk 2L" to "Milk". Light and dark themes follow your device, and the app speaks six languages.

Private, not just promised

Most household apps want an account before you can add a thing. Custoda starts working the moment you open it, and your health information stays on your iPhone.

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Your allergen and diet profile never leaves your device, even when you share with family.
  • Household lists, selected food and saved recipes sync through your own iCloud, only when you use them.
  • Barcode lookups send only the barcode number, never who you are.
  • No ads and no tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Custoda works without sign-up. Your shelf, reminders and allergen and diet profile live on your iPhone. The optional household features use your own iCloud, and only when you turn them on.

Can Custoda tell me a product is safe?

No, and it never claims to. It flags ingredients on your avoid list when product information is available, and says "unverified" when data is missing or incomplete. Product data comes from open, community databases. Always check the packaging. Custoda is not medical advice.

How does household sharing work?

Invite family members with a private iCloud invitation. Everyone shares one shopping list and can suggest recipes and vote on dinner. Sharing food from your inventory is off by default: you choose which items to share and can stop at any time. Your allergen and diet profile is never shared.

Does it track medicines and safety equipment too?

Yes. Keep food and drink, medicines, toiletries and household supplies in one inventory, and save replace-by dates for smoke alarms, carbon-monoxide detectors, fire extinguishers and first-aid supplies.

What does it cost?

Try every feature for 7 days with up to 20 items. After that, keep up to 5 items free, or unlock unlimited storage and the Pro features with a one-time purchase. Allergen warnings stay free. There is no subscription.

Which devices and languages?

Custoda is built for iPhone. The app is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, with light and dark themes that follow your device.
