Jetpack runner

Zoombot

Grab a jetpack, outrun the patrols and rescue your crew across a city of rooftops. Sprint three lanes with one thumb, fly over every danger, beat the bosses and build a robot that is entirely your own.

A city of rooftops

Elevated roads run clean over the rooftops, the sky is wide open, and the sun is low. Then the machines start coming the other way. The road ahead is yours to clear, and your crew is out there waiting to be rescued.

One thumb. Three lanes.

One thumb is all it takes. Your weapon fires itself, so all of your attention goes on reading the road.

Hold and drag for your lane

Three lanes, one thumb. Hold and drag to line up a shot, grab a coin or leave a lane you don't want to be in.

Jump and slide

Swipe up to jump cargo, swipe down to slide under gantries. The road tells you which one is coming.

Your weapon fires itself

The courier shoots on its own, straight down its lane. Choosing the lane is the aiming.

Charge Overdrive

Overdrive charges as you run. Unleash it the moment the road gets mean.

A road that fights back

Every machine that slips past you counts as an escape, and the tenth escape ends the run. You are not running away from anything, you are holding a line.

Bombers, spotters and guardians

Bombers blast you if they slip past, spotters shell whichever lane you are in, and guardians shield the machines around them. Pick your target.

Patrols and armour

Patrols weave between lanes and armoured units shoot back. Heavy machines cost double if they escape. Late in a run, full-width barriers leave no lane to hide in: jump or slide.

Bosses and rescues

Beat rotating bosses to free Echo, Aegis and Zip. But be quick, or the boss escapes with your crewmate.

Three weapon families

Pick the one that suits how you read a lane, then take it further with upgrades and permanent specialisations.

Pulse

Balanced energy bolts, easy to aim and forgiving. The one to learn the road with.

Repeater

Rapid twin fire. Fills a lane with shots and clears clusters before they close on you.

Lance

Heavy rail shots that pierce armour. Slower, but nothing armoured survives a clean line.

Pick-ups change what a lane is worth:

Jetpack: fly over every danger and choose your landing lane Magnets: nearby coins come to you Overdrive: charge it and release it at the right moment

Build your own robot

Your courier is built, not chosen. Every part is earned with coins from the road, and nothing you bolt on changes the difficulty.

  • Mix and match heads, torsos, arms, legs, caps and backpacks.
  • Pick from 30 partners.
  • Choose from four paint coats.
  • Save your favourite looks as named kits.
  • Three weapon families with upgrades and permanent specialisations.

Small crew. Big rescues.

Beat a boss and a stranded crewmate joins you, bringing a power you keep.

  • Echo: every sixth shot pierces and hits 25% harder.
  • Aegis: blocks one hit, then recharges.
  • Zip: recalls three escapes and gives you a burst of rapid fire.

Take one buddy into a run and pair its power with your weapon. Rescue the same buddy three times and it earns its mint trim.

Prove it, then come back

District grades

Every district is graded S to C, and a clean one makes your next coins pay double.

Overclock

Overclock a run with no blue drops, a shorter fuse, rush-hour traffic or a glass core, for up to twice the coins.

A new Daily Run

Every day brings a new road, the same for everyone, with a bonus for reaching district 3.

Free to play. Fair to play.

Coins come from the road, not your wallet, and every upgrade is earnable. Nothing on sale changes enemy health, difficulty or your skill.

An endless mode

An optional purchase that adds a mode without bosses, for players who just want the road.

A coin doubler

A one-time purchase that permanently doubles the coins you earn.

Coin packs

A top-up for when you want that part now. Everything it buys can also just be earned.

Frequently asked questions

When can I play Zoombot?

Right now. Zoombot is out on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Is Zoombot free?

Yes. The game is free to play. Optional purchases add an endless mode without bosses, a permanent coin doubler and coin packs, and every upgrade can be earned by playing.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, no energy timers, no tracking and no account.

How do I control the robot?

One thumb. Hold and drag to pick your lane, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Your weapon fires on its own.

What ends a run?

Ten escapes. Every rogue machine that slips past you counts, and the tenth one ends the run.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Zoombot doesn't collect any data, and purchases are handled by Apple.

Is it violent?

No. It is robots against robots: the rogue machines pop into sparks and bolts, with no people to hurt and no blood.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.
