Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of game is Robot Rooftop Run?

Robot Rooftop Run is an endless runner. You play a delivery courier robot sprinting across a sunlit skyway city, clearing rogue machines out of three lanes, dodging hurdles and gantries, rescuing stranded couriers and taking on boss battles district by district.

How do I control the robot?

Everything works with one thumb. Swipe left or right to change lane, swipe up to jump a hurdle and swipe down to slide under a gantry. Your weapon fires on its own straight down its lane, so choosing the lane is the aiming.

What is Overdrive?

Overdrive charges as you run. When it's ready, tap to release it: your fire pierces all three lanes at once for a few seconds. Holding it for the right moment is worth a lot more than spending it early.

What ends a run?

Ten escapes. Every rogue machine that gets past you counts as an escape, and the tenth one ends the run. Taking damage hurts, but it's the escapes that finish you.

How do I rescue a buddy?

Stranded couriers appear in the road during a run. Reach one and it joins your crew, and you keep its power. Rescue the same buddy three times to earn its mint trim.

What's in the Workshop?

The Workshop is where you build your courier. Head, chest, arms, legs, cap and pack are all separate slots, and every part is bought with coins earned on the road. Tap a part to preview it live on your robot and tap again to equip it. You can save named kits and switch your whole look in one tap, and give your buddies their own skins.

What are the game modes?

Rescue Run is the boss-battle campaign and it's included in the free game. Endless Delivery is an optional, permanent mode unlock that removes boss battles so you can just keep running.

Which devices are supported?

Robot Rooftop Run is made for iPhone and iPad.

Is Robot Rooftop Run free?

Yes. Robot Rooftop Run is free to play, with no ads. Optional in-app purchases are the Endless Delivery mode unlock, a permanent coin doubler and a coin pack. Every part, kit, buddy skin and weapon upgrade can also be earned by playing.

How do I restore purchases?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with and use Restore Purchases in the game. The Endless Delivery unlock and the coin doubler are permanent and can be restored. Coin packs are consumable: once coins are added to your game they are part of your local progress and cannot be restored separately.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Robot Rooftop Run has no accounts and doesn't collect any data. Deleting the game deletes its local progress, subject to your iOS and iCloud backup settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a bug?

Email blueflamegames@proton.me with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, what happened, and the steps to make it happen again. Please don't send passwords or payment details.
