Help and support

Robot Rooftop Run is being prepared for release on iPhone and iPad.

Controls

Hold and drag left or right to change lanes. Swipe up to jump and down to slide. Your weapon fires automatically. Tap Overdrive when charged. Construction blocks require a safe route around them; gates may require a jump or slide.

Endless Delivery

Rescue Run includes boss battles. The optional Endless Delivery purchase permanently adds a mode without boss encounters. Both modes retain obstacles, enemies and weapon upgrades.

Restore a purchase

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Game Modes and choose Restore Purchases. This does not make another purchase. Ask to Buy purchases remain locked until approved. A refunded purchase no longer unlocks the paid mode.

Local progress

Scores, coins and upgrades are stored on the device. Restoring a purchase does not transfer them to another device. Avoid deleting the game while troubleshooting if you want to retain local progress.

Report a problem

Tell us your device model, iOS version, what happened, and the steps needed to reproduce it. Please do not send your Apple password or payment details.
