PhotoGuessr

Remember that trip? PhotoGuessr turns your family's own photo library into a game for the whole couch. Pass the phone around, guess where each picture was taken, and rediscover trips and days you had almost forgotten.

Pass the phone. Rediscover the day.

Somewhere in your library are summers, city breaks and ordinary Sundays nobody has looked at in years. PhotoGuessr brings them back one picture at a time, and gets everyone talking about where you were and who forgot their hat.

Guess together, on one screen

One iPhone or iPad is passed around the couch. There's nothing to set up for the players, just a photo and a map.

Look at the photo

A picture from your own library appears for everyone: “Where was this taken?” The room starts to remember.

Drop a secret pin

Each player takes the phone in turn and places a pin on the map. Nobody else sees it. Add a per-player countdown of 30, 60 or 90 seconds, or play with no timer.

Lock it in

When everyone has locked in, the phone is passed back to the middle of the couch, ready for the big moment.

Reveal it together

Every pin appears on the map with its distance and points, the round winner is crowned, and the photo shows its place name and date.

Who remembers best?

Closer pins score more, up to 5,000 points a round. Switch on the optional bonus to guess the year as well.

  • Every pin on the map, with distance and points
  • A round winner, every round
  • The place name and date of the photo
  • Final standings when the last round ends
  • A family roster with each person's wins, and your recent games

Your photos, your rules

PhotoGuessr only uses pictures that already have a location, and it never shows photos you've hidden.

Choose the memories

Pick which years and albums to play from: the summer of 2019, the big birthday trip, or everything at once.

Set the pace

1 to 8 players, 5, 10 or 15 rounds, and a countdown of Off, 30, 60 or 90 seconds per player.

Put it on the TV

Mirror your iPhone or iPad to the television with AirPlay so the whole family can see the photo and the reveal.

A free gift to families

PhotoGuessr is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no accounts. It's a small present from a small studio, for the family that wants an excuse to look at old photos together.

Frequently asked questions

When can I play PhotoGuessr?

PhotoGuessr is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. We'll link it here as soon as it's live.

Is PhotoGuessr free?

Yes. It's completely free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no account.

Do my photos leave my device?

No. Photos are read on your device and never uploaded or copied anywhere. To show the place name when an answer is revealed, the photo's location (not the photo) is looked up with Apple Maps.

Which photos does it use?

Only photos in your library that have a location. Hidden photos are never shown, and you can limit the game to certain years and albums.

Can we play on the TV?

Yes. Mirror your iPhone or iPad to a TV with AirPlay and everyone can see the photo and the reveal.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.
