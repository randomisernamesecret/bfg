Every run, in your pocket

Record a session, then open it to see the route, splits, elevation and heart rate. Training load and your smart ring are one tab away, and all of it lives on your phone.

What it does

A complete tracker for runs, rides, walks and hikes, with training-load estimates and standard exports, built so your workout history stays yours.

GPS run, ride, walk & hike

Distance, pace, time and splits live, then the route, elevation and heart-rate zones afterwards. Pair a Bluetooth heart-rate strap from the Record screen.

Apple Watch, phone optional

Record from your wrist and leave the phone at home. Watch workouts arrive on your iPhone with splits, personal records and matched routes.

Injury Guard

Estimates your training load against what you're used to, flags risky sessions and can alert you during a workout. A guide to help you judge, not a diagnosis.

Heat alerts, opt-in

Switch on heat alerts and PaceGrid can warn you during a workout when conditions turn dangerously hot, using the Cloudmesh weather service and a rounded location. Off unless you turn it on.

Strava sync, optional

Connect Strava and completed GPS workouts upload automatically; leave it unconnected and nothing goes there. Import GPX, FIT, TCX and zipped Strava exports too.

Routes & offline maps

Save favourite loops, build new routes and download map regions for use offline. Following a saved route works without a connection.

GPX & FIT export

Export any workout in standard formats, so your history is never trapped in one app.

Works with your smart ring

Pair an affordable Colmi-family smart ring straight from PaceGrid — no separate ring app and no account. Your ring's vitals and sleep show up on the dedicated Device tab, with day charts and weekly trends.

All-day vitals

Heart rate, blood oxygen, stress and HRV, read straight from the ring and shown with day charts and a 7-day trend — recovery signals you can actually use before a hard session.

Sleep, properly

A sleep score, the stage breakdown and a hypnogram you can drag across with your finger, plus a 7-night trend. If you wear both, Apple Watch sleep comes first.

Skin temperature

Your overnight skin temperature, shown as a trend against your own typical range so you can spot the nights you ran warmer or cooler.

Direct & private

PaceGrid talks to the ring over Bluetooth itself — no cloud, no login. Ring heart rate can feed a workout, and you can save ring data to Apple Health if you like. Use it alongside Ondi on the same phone.

Supported rings

Tested: COLMI R09. Compatible (same family, not individually tested): COLMI R02, R03, R06, R10 and most rings that pair without their own app. Heart rate and blood oxygen are confirmed across the family; sleep accuracy varies by model.

How to pair

1 · Charge it

Give the ring a few minutes on its charger so it has power to connect.

2 · Quit the ring's own app

If you used the official ring app, fully close it. You can keep PaceGrid and Ondi paired at the same time — just don't sync in two apps at the same moment.

3 · Turn on Bluetooth

Make sure Bluetooth is on and PaceGrid is allowed to use it.

4 · Open the Device tab

Open the Device tab and tap Scan for Ring with the ring next to your phone.

5 · Tap your ring

Choose your ring from the list to connect. PaceGrid remembers it for next time.

6 · Wear it

Wear it day and night — heart rate is a tap away and sleep syncs automatically when you reopen the app.

Train smarter, not just harder

PaceGrid doesn't just count your kilometres — it compares how quickly you're loading up with what your body is used to, so you can spot a risky jump before it becomes a habit.

  • Injury Guard compares your recent load with your longer-term baseline.
  • Live alerts during workouts: heart-rate ceiling and drift, pace fade, duration, hydration and optional heat.
  • Recovery estimates, heart-rate zones, race predictions and adaptive training plans.
  • Estimates to guide you, not a diagnosis or a guarantee.
