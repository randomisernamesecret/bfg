Draft an arsenal. Evolve it. Outlast the horde.

Survive, then keep going

Waves close in from every direction and your weapons fire on their own. Each run escalates from a few stragglers to hundreds on screen, through the Cathedral Hollows, the Plague Quarter, the Eclipse Foundry and the Bellgrave Crypt. Read the danger zones, Phase Step through them, and push past the final boss into Overtime.

Build a run that's yours

Every level-up is a choice, and no two runs of the same hunter end up alike.

A four-weapon arsenal

Level-ups offer other hunters' weapons as well as upgrades. Carry up to three found weapons alongside your own, each with eight levels, and watch them fire together.

Evolutions from treasure

Take a found weapon to level 8, hold its paired passive, and the next treasure chest evolves it into something much louder.

Boss treasure

Bosses drop treasure chests that roll one, three or five upgrades, and elites sometimes drop one too. Follow the golden beam.

Enemies with a plan

Chargers lunge down marked lanes, casters hurl dark blasts, binders ring you in and brutes slam the ground. Every attack shows its danger zone first.

Phase Step & Perfect Dodge

An active dodge with weight. Time it through an attack's telegraph and land a Perfect Dodge for a reward: the game pays out skill, not just running in circles.

Builds that diverge

Synergy passives, plus reroll, banish and lock on every level-up offer, mean two runs of the same hunter play nothing alike.

Pay once. No ads. No timers.

Bellgrave is a premium game that respects your wallet. Buy it once and it's yours. Nothing stands between you and the next run, and nothing nags you mid-fight.

  • One-time unlock: buy it once, play it forever.
  • No ads, no pop-ups, no video rewards to sit through.
  • No battle pass, no season pass, no FOMO timers.
  • No energy system: run as many times as you want.
  • No pay-to-win: every hunter and upgrade is earned in-game.
  • Everything on-device: no account, no login, no sign-up wall.

Every run feeds the next

Four cursed realms, eight multi-phase bosses and a longer game that keeps growing even when you lose.

Four cursed realms

The Cathedral Hollows, the Plague Quarter, the Eclipse Foundry and the Bellgrave Crypt, each with its own look and its own music.

Eight multi-phase bosses

Each boss seals you inside its ritual circle and changes its attacks as it weakens. Learn the patterns, then break them.

Curse Depths

A per-hunter ascension ladder from Surface to Nightfall. Clear a depth, unlock the next, and chase mastery as the run gets meaner.

Mastery Keystones

Permanent per-hunter perks that keep growing even when you lose. A bad run still moves you forward.

Overtime

Killing the final boss doesn't end the run. The horde keeps coming: push your build until you're finally overwhelmed.

Hunter's Chronicle

A completion grid where every run ticks a box, with an Arsenal Codex of every evolution you've found and a Nemesis list that remembers what put you down.

Bragging rights, built in

Hand the exact same run to a friend and dare them to do better.

Seed-share runs

Share a seed code or QR and a friend drops into the exact same setup: same hunter, same realm, same seed. Outplay me.

End-of-run brag cards

Every run ends with a shareable card stamping your hunter, depth and kill count. Proof, not trash talk.

11 hunters

Eleven hunters, each with a signature weapon and a feel of their own, and secret hunters whose weapons join your drafts once you unlock them.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Bellgrave is a one-time unlock with no subscription, no ads, no battle pass and no energy timers.

How do weapon evolutions work?

Draft another hunter's weapon, take it to level 8 and hold its paired passive. The next boss treasure chest evolves it.

How does seed-share work?

Share a seed code or QR and a friend gets the same starting setup, same hunter, realm and seed, then compare brag cards and outplay each other.
