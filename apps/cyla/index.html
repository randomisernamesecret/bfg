Private by design

Ondi

A period, ovulation and cycle tracker that keeps your logs on your phone. No account, no tracking, no Ondi server — with Apple Watch temperature, smart ring sleep and modes for every stage, from first periods to pregnancy and menopause.

Your cycle, at a glance

Log the day in a couple of taps, read your predictions and insights, and watch the picture sharpen with every cycle. None of it needs an account, and nothing here is sent anywhere.

What it does

A complete cycle tracker, from the first log to pregnancy and beyond, built so your most personal health data stays yours.

Cycle predictions

Automatic cycle detection and period predictions that learn from your own history, worked out on your device.

Daily log in seconds

Flow, mood, energy, sleep and symptoms in a couple of taps, a calendar of every cycle, and alerts when something looks irregular.

Apple Watch temperature

With your permission, Ondi reads wrist temperature and heart rate from Apple Health, alongside the temperature, cervical mucus and ovulation tests you log.

Every stage

Trying to Conceive, Pregnancy (week by week, with a due-date window), Postpartum and post-menopause modes, plus perimenopause pattern detection.

Insights in plain language

PMS prediction, headache and migraine patterns, contraception side-effect matching and sleep across your cycle. On supported devices, Ask Ondi answers questions about your own logs, on your iPhone.

No account required

No email, no sign-up, no cloud sync. Open the app and start. Everything is free for 7 days; after that the core tracker stays free and one purchase unlocks the rest.

Works with your smart ring

Pair an affordable Colmi-family smart ring straight from Ondi — no separate ring app and no account. Your ring's heart rate, blood oxygen and sleep appear on a dedicated Device tab, and you decide whether ring sleep is saved to Apple Health.

Everyday vitals

Heart rate, blood oxygen, stress and HRV from the ring, with a day chart and a 7-day view. Measured and kept on your phone — no cloud, no login.

Sleep, at a glance

A sleep score, efficiency, the stage breakdown and a hypnogram you can scrub with your finger, plus your last 7 nights. If you wear an Apple Watch to bed, its sleep comes first.

Temperature, kept separate

The ring's skin temperature is a wellbeing readout only. Your fertility temperature still comes from Apple Watch — we keep the two apart on purpose.

Private by design

Ondi talks to the ring over Bluetooth itself. Ring nights go to Apple Health only if you leave that switch on, and only when no Apple Watch sleep exists for that night. You can use the ring with PaceGrid on the same phone.

Supported rings

Tested: COLMI R09. Compatible (same family, not individually tested): COLMI R02, R03, R06, R10 and most rings that pair without their own app. Heart rate is confirmed across the family; sleep accuracy varies by model.

How to pair

1 · Charge it

Give the ring a few minutes on its charger so it has power to connect.

2 · Quit the ring's own app

If you used the official ring app, fully close it. You can keep Ondi and PaceGrid paired at the same time — just don't sync in two apps at the same moment.

3 · Turn on Bluetooth

Make sure Bluetooth is on and Ondi is allowed to use it.

4 · Open Smart Ring

In Settings, open the Smart Ring screen and tap Scan with the ring next to your phone.

5 · Tap your ring

Choose your ring from the list to connect. Ondi remembers it for next time.

6 · Wear it

Wear it overnight — sleep syncs automatically when you reopen the app.

Private, not just promised

Most trackers ask for an account before you can log a single day, then sync your most intimate data to a vendor cloud. Ondi doesn't.

  • Your logs are stored on your device and encrypted by iOS when it's locked.
  • No account, no Ondi server for your health records, no tracking analytics.
  • Apple Health and smart-ring sync are optional and happen on your device.
  • Discreet notifications and Lock Screen widgets that hide personal details.

Who it's for

Ondi is for people who want a private period tracker, a private ovulation and cycle tracker, or a Flo alternative that does not begin with an account signup. It is especially relevant if privacy is not an abstract preference for you, but the main reason you are choosing a new health app.

No account-first workflow

You do not need to create an account just to log a cycle. That changes the feel of the app immediately.

On-device privacy

Designed to keep sensitive cycle data on the device instead of sending it to a vendor-controlled cloud.

Every stage of life

From first periods to trying to conceive, pregnancy, postpartum and menopause, Ondi switches modes as your life changes.

Export without lock-in

With the unlock, export your full history to a file for a doctor's appointment, so your data stays portable rather than trapped.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ondi private?

Yes. There is no account, no Ondi server for your health records and no tracking analytics. In Settings, network access reads: None.

What's free?

Period tracking, daily logs, predictions, the calendar and irregularity alerts, always. Everything is free for 7 days; then one purchase unlocks fertility, every mode, insights and export. No subscription.
