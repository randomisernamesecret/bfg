Your cycle, at a glance

Log the day in a couple of taps, see your estimates and your own patterns, and keep a diary of every cycle. None of it needs an account, and nothing you log is sent to us.

What it does

A personal cycle diary, from the first log to pregnancy and beyond, built so your most personal data stays yours.

Next-period estimate

Automatic cycle detection and a next-period estimate with a range and how sure it is, worked out on your device from your own logged cycles. Estimates can be wrong.

Daily log in seconds

Flow, mood, energy, sleep and symptoms in a couple of taps, a calendar of every cycle, and a note when your logged cycles vary a lot.

Apple Watch temperature

With your permission, Ondi reads wrist temperature and sleep from Apple Health and shows them as trends next to your log. You can also log temperature, cervical mucus and ovulation tests as notes.

Every stage

Pregnancy, postpartum and post-menopause diaries, in plain language. In pregnancy mode the due date is an estimate worked out from the date you enter; your midwife or doctor gives you your real dates.

Insights in plain language

Notes on what you logged, such as PMS patterns, headaches and sleep across your cycle, with a reminder to talk to a doctor. On supported devices, Ask Ondi answers questions about your own logs, on your iPhone.

No account required

No email, no sign-up, no Ondi server. Open the app and start. Everything is free for 7 days; after that logging, history and the calendar stay free and one purchase unlocks the rest.

Works with your smart ring

Pair an affordable Colmi-family smart ring straight from Ondi — no separate ring app and no account. Your ring's heart rate, blood oxygen and sleep appear on a dedicated Device tab, and you decide whether ring sleep is saved to Apple Health.

Everyday vitals

Heart rate, blood oxygen, stress and HRV from the ring, with a day chart and a 7-day view. Measured and kept on your phone — no Ondi server, no login.

Sleep, at a glance

Sleep efficiency, the stage breakdown and a hypnogram you can scrub with your finger, plus your last 7 nights. If you wear an Apple Watch to bed, its sleep comes first.

Temperature, kept separate

The ring's skin temperature is a readout only. Wrist temperature trends come from Apple Watch — we keep the two apart on purpose.

Private by design

Ondi talks to the ring over Bluetooth itself. Ring nights go to Apple Health only if you turn that switch on, and only when no Apple Watch sleep exists for that night. You can use the ring with PaceGrid on the same phone.

Supported rings

Tested: COLMI R09. Compatible (same family, not individually tested): COLMI R02, R03, R06, R10 and most rings that pair without their own app. Heart rate is confirmed across the family; sleep accuracy varies by model.

How to pair

1 · Charge it

Give the ring a few minutes on its charger so it has power to connect.

2 · Quit the ring's own app

If you used the official ring app, fully close it. You can keep Ondi and PaceGrid paired at the same time — just don't sync in two apps at the same moment.

3 · Turn on Bluetooth

Make sure Bluetooth is on and Ondi is allowed to use it.

4 · Open Smart Ring

In Settings, open the Smart Ring screen and tap Scan with the ring next to your phone.

5 · Tap your ring

Choose your ring from the list to connect. Ondi remembers it for next time.

6 · Wear it

Wear it overnight — sleep syncs automatically when you reopen the app.

Private, not just promised

Most trackers ask for an account before you can log a single day, then sync your most intimate data to the vendor's servers. Ondi doesn't.

  • Your logs are stored on your device and encrypted by iOS when it's locked.
  • No account, no Ondi server for your health records, no tracking analytics.
  • Apple Health and smart-ring features are optional and run on your device; saving periods to Apple Health is off until you turn it on.
  • Discreet notifications and Lock Screen widgets that hide personal details.

Who it's for

Ondi is for people who want a private period tracker, a personal cycle diary, or a period app that does not begin with an account signup. It is especially relevant if privacy is not an abstract preference for you, but the main reason you are choosing a new health app.

No account-first workflow

You do not need to create an account just to log a cycle. That changes the feel of the app immediately.

On-device privacy

Designed to keep sensitive cycle data on the device instead of sending it to a vendor's servers.

Every stage of life

From first periods to pregnancy, postpartum and menopause, Ondi has a diary for each stage, and you switch modes as your life changes.

Export without lock-in

Export your full history to a file for a doctor's appointment, so your data stays portable rather than trapped.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ondi private?

Yes. There is no account, no Ondi server and no tracking analytics. Your logs are stored on your device, and device backups follow Apple's rules. The only network request is an App Store lookup for the names and icons of our other apps; it carries no health data.

What's free?

Logging in every mode, your full history, the calendar, the next-period estimate and export of your own data, always. Everything is free for 7 days; then one purchase unlocks insights, Ask Ondi on supported devices, smart ring insights and the pregnancy, postpartum and post-menopause dashboards. No subscription.
