What it does

A complete primary maths companion: curriculum topics, times tables practice and short games that keep children coming back.

Reception to Year 6

More than 50 topics, from number and the four operations to fractions, measurement, geometry and statistics, matched to your child's year group.

Times tables 2–12

Practise every table, earn stars per table, see a heat map of what's secure, race the clock in Table Race and try Ready Mode: 25 questions, 6 seconds each.

Adaptive difficulty

Difficulty follows how the last few sessions went, and a daily plan mixes new topics with reviews.

Spaced review

Topics come back for review after a day or up to a week, depending on how secure they are, so learning sticks.

Games that build fluency

Wonder Trail's 50 levels, a 60-second Sprint and four challenge modes: Speed Rush, Flawless Run, Combo Blitz and Boss Battle.

A pet to care for

Practice earns coins to feed and look after Pip and personalise an avatar, which gives children a reason to come back tomorrow.

Built around the UK curriculum

Arithmetix follows what children in England are expected to learn, year by year, so parents can see what is being practised. Younger children get visual activities and large answer buttons. Older children get an on-screen scratch pad to work things out.

  • Reception to Year 6, organised by year group.
  • MTC-style Ready Mode for Year 4 times tables.
  • Hints and visual questions with bar charts and clocks.
  • Free for 7 days, then one purchase. No subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in, email or password. Child profiles, answers and progress stay on the device, and a Parent PIN protects settings and purchases.

Does it help with times tables?

Yes. Tables 2 to 12 have their own practice area with a heat map, Table Race and an MTC-style Ready Mode for Year 4.

How does pricing work?

Every feature is free for 7 days. After that, a single purchase unlocks the full app for all your child profiles. No subscription, no ads.
